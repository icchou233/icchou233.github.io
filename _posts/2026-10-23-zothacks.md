Over this past weekend, I attended my first ever hackathon; which is ZotHacks.
I'm very grateful I got in, as this hackathon has been quite a learning experience for me.
While I have made a simple blog/portfolio website, I've never learned how to program APIs or a backend server. 
In summary, I recommend every UCI student who has never done a hackathon to apply.
They do only take absolute beginners at web development.

**Friday 10/16**

All of us were preassigned into teams beforehand, with a mentor assigned to each team.
After all of us did a round of icebreakers, we spent some time on ideation before all of us quickly agreed to pursue a Spotify play generator idea,
which we called the MoodMixer.
I worked on the backend portion of the website, as I liked solving logical problems more than UI/UX design.
We spent the rest of the day individually trying to learn as many web development concepts as possible.

**Saturday 10/17**

Our hacking period is 10 am - 10 pm, with free food included in between. 
There's not much to say about what happened during this time period other than that for 15 minutes during the hacking period 
our team thought we were done with our website before finding out there were still issues with it.
The unfortunate part is that we didn't get a fully functional website done on time, as the frontend-backend integration had issues.
3 of us decided to stay behind after the hacking period to try to fix our issue; 30 minutes after the deadline, we got our website to work.
(We already submitted a zip file of our code to the judges before the deadline, so this didn't count for judging.)

**Sunday 10/18**

After a round of judging, we had our awards ceremony.
The first place project was SignLingo (website that helps you learn ASL), with the runner-up being CircleBack (behavioral interview practice).
The Hacker's choice award was PeterParker (website that helps you find a parking space at UCI).
We obviously didn't win, but that was quite obvious from the start, and the other teams turned in pretty good projects too.
